HOW TO PLAY

  • Mobile: Swipe Left/Right to switch lanes, Swipe Up to jump, Swipe Down to slide.
  • Desktop: Arrow Keys or A/D to switch lanes, Space/Up to jump, Down to slide, Enter to restart.
  • Power-Ups: Magnet pulls fish, Shield blocks 1 crash, 2x Fish doubles coins, Speed Boost blasts ahead!
  • Watch Out: Stumbling lets the angry bulldog catch up!

SETTINGS

INSTALL ON ANDROID

  • Open this game in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  • Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  • Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Launch Cat Escape from your home screen — works offline too!
